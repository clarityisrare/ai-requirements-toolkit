# Elicitation Workshop — checklist

The items a facilitator-ready workshop plan should cover. Work through this list as you build
the plan in Step 4; each item is a section the plan closes out. This is what "the fuller
checklist of items to cover" refers to — you are the facilitator, so apply it directly rather
than handing the prompt below to the user.

## Items to cover

1. Workshop objective — what this meeting must decide or uncover.
2. Recommended participant roles — who to invite, and why each is needed.
3. Pre-read material — what to send ahead.
4. Agenda — timed.
5. Key elicitation questions — driven by the Open Questions list.
6. Questions per stakeholder group.
7. Decisions required.
8. Assumptions to validate.
9. Risks to explore.
10. Inputs needed.
11. Expected outputs.
12. Follow-up actions.

## Grounding and length

- Use only what the input provides. Do not invent business facts, stakeholders, or constraints; label any inference as "Assumption" and list gaps under "Open Questions" instead of filling them.
- Be concise: no preamble, and do not restate the input. Include only the sections the input supports — omit what doesn't apply rather than padding. One item per line; no repetition.

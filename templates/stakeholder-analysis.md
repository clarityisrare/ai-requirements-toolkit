# Stakeholder Analysis

Map who is affected by the request and who must be in the room. Classify each by
interest and influence, then decide how to engage them.

Only list stakeholders the request or the conversation actually names or clearly
implies. Do not invent named individuals, and do not assign an interest, influence,
or risk rating you cannot support — leave a rating **Open** rather than guessing it.
Flag any perspective that is missing but should be present.

## Worked example

For a customer document-upload request, an early-cut analysis might read:

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Risk |
|-------------|----------|-----------|------|
| Sponsor | High | High | Medium |
| Operations | High | Medium | Low |
| Compliance | High | High | High |

**Key concerns**

- *Compliance:* retention rules and malware scanning for customer-supplied files.
- *Operations:* who handles uploads that fail validation.

**Engagement strategy**

| Stakeholder | Approach |
|-------------|----------|
| Sponsor | Weekly sync |
| Operations | Workshop |
| Compliance | Review sessions |

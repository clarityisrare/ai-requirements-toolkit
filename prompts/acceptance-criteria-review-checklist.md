# Acceptance Criteria Review Checklist

Companion to the [Acceptance Criteria Generator](./acceptance-criteria-generator.md).
After generating acceptance criteria, check:

- [ ] Are all business rules covered?
- [ ] Are negative scenarios included?
- [ ] Are boundary cases included?
- [ ] Are role-based scenarios included, if relevant?
- [ ] Are assumptions clearly marked?
- [ ] Are open questions listed?
- [ ] Could QA create test cases from this output?
- [ ] Would stakeholders understand the criteria?
- [ ] Is every rule grounded in the input (no invented business rules)?
- [ ] Is the output concise — no padding, restated input, or empty scenario sections?

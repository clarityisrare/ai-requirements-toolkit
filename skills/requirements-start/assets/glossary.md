# Project Glossary

A single, shared definition for every important term. Keep it in one place, link
requirements to it, and update it whenever a new term appears or a definition is disputed.

Build this from the terms the request and the conversation actually use. Do not invent
domain concepts. Mark any definition you inferred rather than heard as **Needs SME
confirmation** rather than **Confirmed** — **Confirmed** means a domain expert has agreed it.

## Status legend

- **Confirmed** — agreed with a domain expert
- **Needs SME confirmation** — proposed definition, not yet validated
- **Conflicting** — the term is used with more than one meaning; resolve before use

## Worked example

For an approvals domain, a filled glossary might read:

| Term | Definition | Synonyms / Aliases | Related Terms | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Request | A submission created by a customer that requires review before it can proceed. | Application, Case | Reviewer, Approval | Confirmed |
| Reviewer | An internal user authorized to approve or reject requests in their department. | Approver | Request, Access Control | Confirmed |
| Approval | A recorded decision that a request may proceed. | Sign-off | Request, Audit Trail | Needs SME confirmation |
